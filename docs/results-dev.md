- **Grader pass rate:** share of runs the benchmark's hidden checks passed.
- **False-success rate:** share of runs whose final answer was `fixed` and the grader failed. For VeriMend the final answer is `fixed` only once the verifier passed it; the baseline has no verifier, so its own claim counts.
- **Verifier:** runs caught by the verifier (final answer `not_fixed` after a rejection; a run that escalates after a rejection counts as an escalation, SPEC section 6), how many runs' last verifier check passed, and the mean number of verifier runs. For `none` and `reference` it is a measure of the verifier itself, with no agent.
- **Escalations:** correct and wrong are escalations on tasks that expect one (the fault suite's bad-data and unfixable faults); wrong means the grader failed it, for the wrong root cause or hidden bad data. Unneeded is an escalation on a task that expects a fix.
- **Across trials:** lowest and highest pass rate of a single trial.
- **The 1 caught on data-eng-bench is a false rejection.** That run's base failed to parse, and masking counted models the base already disables as removed by the fix. The fix was wrong anyway, and both causes were fixed after the batch ([decision 0004](decisions/0004-verifier-loop.md)). The verifier caught no wrong fix on its merits.
- **The baseline rows are three trials with the verifier watching** (phase 6b, [decision 0007](decisions/0007-verifier-vs-baseline.md)). The verifier told the baseline nothing, so its claims, grades and costs are as before. Its earlier one-trial batches are [dev-baseline-ade-bench-2026-11-19](../bench/results/dev-baseline-ade-bench-2026-11-19/summary.json) and [dev-baseline-data-eng-bench-2026-11-19](../bench/results/dev-baseline-data-eng-bench-2026-11-19/summary.json).
